💡 What is InkPanel?

InkPanel is a tiny demo webcomics library — a single static website with a vertical-scroll reader, just like your favourite webtoon apps. Everything runs in the browser: bookmarks and theme are saved on your device, no account needed.

  • ✅ Free forever, no sign-up
  • ✅ Mobile-friendly vertical reader
  • ✅ Dark mode + personal library
  • ✅ New episodes every Friday

✍️ Are you a creator?

Fork this site on GitHub, drop your episodes into assets/images/episodes/, add an entry to comics.js — and you have your own webcomics site.
